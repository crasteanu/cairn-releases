Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Cairn is designed to run entirely on your Mac. We do not collect analytics, crash telemetry, filenames, directory listings, or personal data.

1. Local-Only Scanning & Cleanup

All disk scans, duplicate checks, similar-photo Vision analysis, app inventories, and privacy audits happen locally on your Mac. File paths and scan results never leave your device.

2. License Activation (Optional)

If you purchase Cairn Pro and enter a license key, Cairn sends only your license key, a machine label, and our Polar organization identifier to Polar.sh (api.polar.sh) to activate, validate, or deactivate your license. Payment processing, invoicing, and VAT handling are performed by Polar Software, Inc. as Merchant of Record.

3. Update Checks (Optional)

When checking for updates, Cairn fetches a static XML appcast from GitHub Pages (crasteanu.github.io) and verifies downloads locally using an embedded Ed25519 public key. No personal identifiers are sent.

4. Support Diagnostics

If you choose to export a Support Diagnostics report from Cairn's Settings, personal paths are redacted automatically before you decide whether to share it with us.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: claudiurasteanu@icloud.com
